Street Dog Games

In early 2024 I had an idea for a game. The real world is a challenging place, full of things that can get you down. I wanted a game where I could spend fifteen minutes a day doing something that lifted me up. I love dogs. Helping dogs lifts me up. So I started learning Unity, and began working on the idea.

Sandy and ARC9

Relax. Wander an interesting world. Help dogs.

An open-world exploration game where you find stray dogs and give them treats. That’s it.

Have fifteen minutes between meetings? Help a dog. Sitting on the train commuting? Help a dog. Procrastinating from doing the dishes? Help a dog. No combat, no frustrating puzzles.

The game is nearing a proper alpha release, with an open beta hopefully ready by the end of 2026.
