The following games are the work of Greg Warden. How I use AI.

In early 2024 I had an idea for a game. The real world is a challenging place, full of things that can get you down. I wanted a game where I could spend fifteen minutes a day doing something that lifted me up. I love dogs. Helping dogs lifts me up. So I started learning Unity, and began working on the idea.

Sandy and ARC9

Love dogs? Tired of doomscrolling? Play Sandy and ARC9.

Explore a 100 square mile open world inspired by the beauty of coastal Maine, searching for and befriending street dogs.

No combat. No frustrating puzzles.

Early access coming soon.

Credits

The following external sources provide assets and/or data used in the game:

OpenStreetMap contributors
The town is based on Camden, Maine (one of my favourite places to vacation). Its streets and their names, the coastline and rivers, and where every building stands — map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence.
Microsoft US Building Footprints
The outline of many buildings where OpenStreetMap has none. Contains information from Microsoft US Building Footprints, which is made available here under the Open Database Licence.
Meta & World Resources Institute — High Resolution Canopy Height Maps
Where every tree stands, and how tall it grows. Clipped to the town, resampled onto its grid, and turned into tree cover and height. Licensed under CC BY 4.0.
U.S. Geological Survey — 3D Elevation Program
The shape of the land, and the hills and islands beyond the edge of town. I’m looking at you, Mt Battie. United States Geological Survey (2021), 3D Elevation Program 1/3 arc-second and 1 arc-second Digital Elevation Models, distributed by OpenTopography. Accessed 2026-09-10.
OpenTopography
This work is based on data services provided by the OpenTopography Facility with support from the National Science Foundation under NSF Award Numbers 2410799, 2410800 & 2410801.
Library of Congress — Sanborn Maps Collection
The 1912 fire insurance maps, for a few old downtown buildings no modern source has.
Camden Historic Resources Committee and the National Register of Historic Places
When the houses were built, and their storeys, materials and styles.
Synty Studios
Trees, dogs and animation: POLYGON Nature Biomes and Dog; Animation Base Locomotion; Animation Emotes and Taunts.
Staggart Creations — Stylized Water 3
The water.
Epidemic Sound
The music: Mandala Dreams — Breath of the Ocean and Transition in Mood; Ingrid Witt — If I Had A Tail (Instrumental Version).

Lucky!

Keep Baby happy. Keep Baby alive.

You are Lucky, a robot dog and trusted steed of the last baby on earth. By day, Baby wants pie, soda or ice cream — one thing at a time, and right now. By night, the zombies come out.

A spooky Western comedy, made in October 2026 for the Synty Spooky Jam. It plays in your browser on itch.io.

Visual assets from Synty Studios. Music and most sound effects from Epidemic Sound.
