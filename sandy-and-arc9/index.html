Sandy and ARC9

Relax and befriend streetdogs.

A cozy open-world exploration game where you can feel good about yourself, because you helped some streetdogs.

You play ARC9, a robot. Sandy is a street dog. You wander a small coastal town, and the dogs decide for themselves whether to trust you.

It is being made slowly, by one person, and there is nothing to download yet. This page will grow a changelog when there is something worth writing down.
